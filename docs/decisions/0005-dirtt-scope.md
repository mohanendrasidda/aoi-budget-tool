# ADR 0005: DIRTT interior construction is a separate line, not part of the furniture total

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Decider:** Sidd (repo admin); to confirm with the partner

## Context

The partner is a DIRTT Construction Partner as well as a furniture dealer. DIRTT sells
prefabricated interior construction (solid and glass walls, doors, headwalls, millwork, pre-wired
power). The question is whether the budget tool's furniture range should include it.

Public evidence (sources and access dates in the team's research notes):

- **How it is priced:** each project is quoted in DIRTT's own design software from wall length,
  glazing, doors and power, through a Construction Partner. No public price book exists.
- **How it is classified:** construction in the specification system (MasterFormat 13 40 00,
  formerly 10 22 19 demountable partitions); federal purchases are coded about two-thirds as
  furniture and one-third as prefabricated building components or construction; tax treatment
  (5-7 year personal property vs 39-year real property) is decided per project.
- **How big it is:** on projects that use it, DIRTT-published cases put it at about 25-30% of
  total fit-out cost, roughly the same size as the furniture budget; public figures range from
  about $19 to $67 per sf of floor area, or about $600-1,100 per linear foot installed.
- **How the partner is organized:** its public site lists construction, furniture and
  pre-manufactured interiors as separate groups.

## Decision

1. The furniture range **excludes** DIRTT. Benchmarks, price layer and accuracy scoring stay
   furniture only.
2. The app may later show DIRTT as a **separate, off-by-default line**, turned on per project,
   labeled "allowance required, quoted separately", with the wide public range above and no
   claim of accuracy.
3. No DIRTT pricing model is built until the partner shares past DIRTT quotes with floor area
   and wall length.

## Consequences

- Furniture $/sf benchmarks are not distorted by an item that does not scale with headcount.
- A seller still sees that walls are a separate budget conversation.
- Open: the partner confirms whether DIRTT and furniture are one contract or two, and whether
  the client sees one budget or two.
