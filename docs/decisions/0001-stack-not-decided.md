# 0001: Tech stack is open; build against synthetic data first

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Deciders: team

## Context
Discovery interview (2026-09-17) and showroom visit (2026-09-24) defined the MVP: scope + sq ft in, budget range + $/sq ft out. No AOI data or data agreement yet.

## Decision
Keep the repo stack-agnostic until the team picks a stack. All development uses `data/synthetic/` until AOI confirms in writing what data we may use and where.

## Consequences
Pricing model accuracy can't be validated until real data terms exist. Ask AOI early.
